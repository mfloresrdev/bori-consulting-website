Why AI ProServices exists

Most small businesses in Indiana are still running on word of mouth, a Facebook page, or nothing at all — while their customers expect to book, order, and get quotes online. AI ProServices exists to close that gap: real, custom-built websites and AI agents, priced for a business just starting to invest in its online presence.

How we work

This is a one-person studio, which means every project gets direct attention — no account managers, no handoffs between teams. It also means code quality and security have to hold up without a team behind them, so every build follows a strict internal security and engineering checklist before it ships.
