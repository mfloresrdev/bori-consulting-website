## Business registration (Indiana, informational — not legal/tax advice)
- Decide sole proprietorship vs LLC (LLC recommended once handling client payments/contracts; confirm with an accountant).
- Optional name reservation via INBiz: $10, 120 days.
- Registered agent needed (can be self if IN address).
- File Articles of Organization (Form 49459) via INBiz: $97, ~24hr online processing.
- EIN: free, instant online at irs.gov.
- Open business bank account after EIN.
- Check local/industry licenses via INBiz.
- Biennial Business Entity Report due every 2 years in anniversary month.
